Reading measures
================

The word-level and whole-trial measures computed by
:func:`pyemread.cal.compute_word_measures` and
:func:`pyemread.cal.cal_write_em` are defined in the documentation of
:mod:`pyemread.cal`. Sentence-level measures are computed by
:func:`pyemread.cal.compute_sentence_measures` and
:func:`pyemread.cal.cal_write_sm`; sentences are taken from an optional
``sentence_id`` column of the region file, or segmented at sentence-final
punctuation.
